## How it fills itself in

Every gift you [record](/giving/recording-a-gift) creates an invoice here automatically. You do not enter a donation twice — once in the CRM, once in the books.

The same is true of a [confirmed event payment](/ministry/events).

## What you get

- Every recorded gift as an invoice, with a receipt number
- Totals your treasurer can reconcile against your payment accounts
- Exports for your accountant or your annual return

## Reconciling

Because Harvest never sees your money, your books are only as complete as your recording.

<Steps>
  <Step title="Open your payment accounts">
    PayPal, Revolut, whatever you use.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Compare against your Harvest total">
    Anything in your account but not in Harvest is a gift nobody recorded.
  </Step>

  <Step title="Record the gap">
    It then appears in your books, on your dashboard, and in the member's own statement.
  </Step>
</Steps>

<Tip>
  Doing this weekly takes minutes. Doing it in January takes a day and you will have forgotten who half the payments were from.
</Tip>

## What Harvest is not

Harvest keeps the record of what your church received. It is not a full ledger — it does not track your expenses, your payroll or your gift aid claims.

<Note>
  For most small churches, Harvest plus a spreadsheet for expenses is enough. For anything more, export from Harvest into your accounting software.
</Note>
