You can add as many as you like. Members choose whichever suits them.

## Adding a link

Go to **Donations** and add the public link for each account — your PayPal.me address, your Revolut link, and so on.

<Tip>
  Add at least two. Some of your congregation will have one and not the other, and a second option costs you nothing.
</Tip>

## Where they appear

Once added, your links show up:

- In the member app, under **Give**
- On a fundraising campaign's page
- On a paid event's registration page, if you chose that provider for the event

## Choosing providers per event

When you create a paid event you pick which of your links apply to it. You might take conference tickets through Revolut only, and general giving through everything.

## What Harvest does not do

<Warning>
  Harvest cannot verify that a payment arrived, cannot see amounts, and cannot refund anyone. Your payment provider is the source of truth for all three.

  If a member says they gave and your PayPal disagrees, **your PayPal is right**.
</Warning>

## After a gift arrives

Record it so it counts. [Recording a gift](/giving/recording-a-gift) takes about thirty seconds per gift and keeps every total in Harvest correct.
