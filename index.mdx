## What you get

Every church on Harvest gets its own address — `yourchurch.theharvest.app` — with two sides to it.

**The admin side** is where your team works: your people, your courses, your events, your giving, your Sunday.

**The member app** is what your congregation opens: the news feed, your courses, the Bible, prayer requests, the church map, and a place to give.

## What Harvest does not do

Being honest about this saves you time later.

<Warning>
  **Harvest does not process card payments.** Your church uses its own payment links — PayPal, Revolut, Wise, Venmo, Cash App or Zelle — and the money goes straight to you. Harvest never sees it and never takes a cut. [How giving works](/giving/how-giving-works) explains what that means day to day.
</Warning>

Harvest takes **0% of your donations**, on every plan. Our revenue is the subscription and nothing else.
