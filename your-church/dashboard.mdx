Your dashboard is the first thing you see. It answers the questions you would otherwise go looking for.

## The numbers

Across the top: members, contacts, published courses, community posts, events, form submissions and receipts.

<Note>
  Every figure is either an exact count or nothing at all. Harvest does not show an estimate and call it a number — if a figure cannot be counted honestly, the card says so instead.
</Note>

## Insights

Below the numbers, Harvest writes what changed in words — *"Nobody has given for four weeks running"* rather than a chart you have to interpret.

These are comparisons against your own previous weeks, not benchmarks against other churches.

<Tip>
  The insight worth acting on is usually the run-length one. A single quiet week is noise; four in a row is a pattern.
</Tip>

## The giving figure and what it means

<Warning>
  Your giving total counts **recorded** gifts. Money sitting in your PayPal that nobody has entered does not appear.

  If your dashboard says £0 and you know people gave, the gifts are waiting to be [recorded](/giving/recording-a-gift).
</Warning>

## What you will not find here

Harvest does not rank you against other churches, and does not tell you what a healthy number looks like. Those comparisons are almost always misleading — a rural congregation of forty and a city church of four hundred are not doing the same job.

The dashboard shows you your own church, over time.
