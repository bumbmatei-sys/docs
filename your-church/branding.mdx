Your congregation should open the app and recognise it as yours, not as ours.

## What you can change

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Your logo" icon="image">
    Appears in the member app header and on your public pages.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Your accent colour" icon="palette">
    Buttons, highlights and links pick it up throughout.
  </Card>

Set both under **Branding**.

## Light and dark

Harvest has a light and a dark theme, and members choose which they prefer. Your accent colour works in both.

<Tip>
  Upload a logo that reads on both a white and a dark background. A dark-ink logo on a dark theme disappears — a version with a light outline, or a transparent PNG with enough contrast, saves you the problem.
</Tip>

## What stays the same

Layout, navigation and typography are consistent across every church on Harvest. That is deliberate: it means the app is tested, accessible and fast for everyone, and a member who moves between two Harvest churches is not relearning it.

You are branding the app, not rebuilding it.

## Your address

Your church's address — `yourchurch.theharvest.app` — is set when you create your church and is part of your identity. It appears on registration links, QR codes and anything you share publicly.
