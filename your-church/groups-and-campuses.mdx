## Community groups

<Note>
  Community groups are a **Ministry plan** feature.
</Note>

Groups are the smaller rooms inside your church — a home group, a youth team, a prayer circle, a leadership cohort.

Each group has its own space. Members see the groups they belong to, and nothing they do not.

### Why groups rather than one feed

A whole-church [community feed](/content/blog-and-community) is for the things everyone needs. Groups are for the things a few people need often — the Tuesday study, the worship team's rota chat, the deacons' conversation.

Put both in one feed and people stop reading either.

### Setting one up

Create the group, add its members, and it appears for them in the member app. An admin can add or remove people at any time.

## Campuses

If your church meets in more than one place, each location can be its own campus.

<Warning>
  **Forever Free has no campuses.** Every paid plan — Individual, Small Team and Ministry — can add as many campuses as it needs. There is no campus add-on.

  A second congregation used to be sold as a pack. That pack is gone. Paying at all is what unlocks unlimited locations.
</Warning>

### What a campus gets

Its own identity within your church — so a member knows which service they are looking at, and your team can plan each location separately without running two accounts.

### When you do not need one

If you meet twice on a Sunday in the same building, that is two [services](/ministry/service-planning), not two campuses. Campuses are for separate locations.
