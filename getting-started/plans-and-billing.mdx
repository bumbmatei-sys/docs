Four plans. Every paid plan starts with a **14-day free trial**, and you can pay monthly, quarterly or yearly — quarterly saves 10%, yearly saves over 20%.

Current prices are on [theharvest.site](https://theharvest.site).

## The limits

|  | Forever Free | Individual | Small Team | Ministry |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **Contacts** | 500 | 500 | 2,000 | 4,000 |
| **Courses** | 1 | 2 | 5 | 15 |
| **Admin seats** | 1 | 2 | 5 | 15 |
| **Campuses** | — | Unlimited | Unlimited | Unlimited |

<Note>
  Individual's contact cap matches Forever Free's 500 on purpose. Individual is bought for capability — the feed, the CRM, a donation page — not for more people. Small Team and Ministry are the tiers that raise the cap.
</Note>

## Add-ons

Some limits can be raised without changing plan:

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Unlimited contacts" icon="infinity">
    Removes the contact cap entirely. Ministry only.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Admin seats" icon="user-plus">
    +1 admin each, on any paid plan.
  </Card>
</CardGroup>

[Ask Harvest](/member-app/ask-harvest), the AI study assistant, is also an add-on rather than part of any plan. It is not included in Forever Free, Individual, Small Team or Ministry.

There is no campus add-on. Every paid plan can add as many campuses as it needs. Forever Free has no campuses — it is one evangelist, not a multi-site church.

There is no contacts +500 pack. The caps above already include the raise that pack used to buy.

## Donations are never charged

Harvest takes **0% of your giving** on every plan, including Free. No application fee, no revenue share.

This is structural, not a promotion — your giving goes through [your own payment links](/giving/payment-links) and never touches us.

## Early bird pricing is held

<Note>
  If you join Ministry at the early bird price, you stay at that price. Raising the price later applies to new subscriptions only — it does not move anyone already subscribed.
</Note>

## Changing plan

Upgrade or downgrade from your billing settings at any time.

<Warning>
  **Cancelling ends your subscription immediately, not at the end of the period you have paid for.** If you cancel mid-month you lose the rest of that month. Downgrade instead if you want to keep access until your term ends.
</Warning>

## When you hit a limit

Harvest tells you at the moment you hit it, not afterwards.

If you go over a limit by downgrading — say you have eight courses and move to Small Team's five — **you keep what you have**. You just cannot add more until you are under the cap again.

Sections your plan does not include are **hidden entirely** rather than shown locked. You will not find a nav full of buttons that lead to an upgrade prompt.
