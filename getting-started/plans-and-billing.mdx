Harvest has four plans. Every paid plan comes with a **14-day free trial**, and you can pay monthly, quarterly or yearly — quarterly saves 10% and yearly saves over 20%.

## The plans

<CardGroup cols={2}>
  <Card title="Forever Free" icon="seedling">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Plus" icon="users">

  </Card>

  <Card title="Pro" icon="building-church">
    For a growing church that needs more room and more of the ministry tools.
  </Card>

  <Card title="Ministry" icon="crown">
    For established churches going deeper. Currently at an **early bird price**.
  </Card>

The full comparison, with current prices, is on [theharvest.site](https://theharvest.site).

<Note>
  **Early bird pricing is held.** If you join Ministry at the early bird price, you stay at that price. Raising the price later affects new subscriptions only — it does not move anyone already subscribed.
</Note>

## Donations are never charged

Harvest takes **0% of your giving** on every plan, including Free. There is no application fee and no revenue share. Our income is the subscription and nothing else.

This is not a promotion — it is how Harvest is built. Your giving goes through [your own payment links](/giving/payment-links) and never touches us.

## Changing plan

You can upgrade or downgrade from your billing settings at any time.

<Warning>
  **Cancelling ends your subscription immediately, not at the end of the period you have paid for.** If you cancel mid-month, you lose the rest of that month. Downgrade instead if you want to keep access until your term ends.
</Warning>

## What happens when a plan limit is reached

Each plan includes a number of courses, admin seats and churches. When you reach a limit, Harvest tells you at the moment you hit it rather than failing quietly.

Sections your plan does not include are **hidden entirely** rather than shown as locked. You will not find a nav full of buttons that lead to an upgrade prompt.

## Your data is yours

If you leave, you can export your members and your giving records. See [Data and privacy](/account/data-and-privacy).
