<Update label="18 September 2026" tags={["Plans"]}>
  ## Campuses are unlimited on every paid plan

  The campus add-on is gone. Individual, Small Team and Ministry can each add as many locations as they need. Forever Free still has none — it is one evangelist, not a multi-site church.

  Contact caps moved at the same time, and the old Contacts +500 pack went with the campus pack:

  - Individual: 150 → **500** (same cap as Forever Free)
  - Small Team: 500 → **2,000**
  - Ministry: 2,000 → **4,000**

  Unlimited contacts remains an add-on, Ministry only. Extra admin seats still stack. Ask Harvest is still an add-on, on any paid plan — it is not included in Ministry.
</Update>
