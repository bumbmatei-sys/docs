<Update label="18 September 2026" tags={["Plans"]}>
  ## Campuses are unlimited on every paid plan

  The campus add-on is gone. Individual, Small Team and Ministry can each add as many locations as they need. Forever Free still has none — it is one evangelist, not a multi-site church.

  Contact caps moved at the same time, and the old Contacts +500 pack went with the campus pack:

  - Individual: 150 → **500** (same cap as Forever Free)
  - Small Team: 500 → **2,000**
  - Ministry: 2,000 → **4,000**

  Unlimited contacts remains an add-on, Ministry only. Extra admin seats still stack. Ask Harvest is still an add-on, on any paid plan — it is not included in Ministry.
</Update>

<Update label="15 September 2026" tags={["Design","Fixed"]}>
  ## Your logo disappeared on the dark theme

  Ten screens show your church's logo. Only two of them handled a dark background — so on the other eight, a church with a dark-ink logo showed almost nothing.

  Seven of those eight were the pages your congregation and the public actually see: giving, pledges, campaigns, event registration, check-in, forms and the calendar. **None of them forces light mode**, so a visitor with dark mode switched on saw a near-black logo on a near-black page.

  **Fixed.** Every surface now puts a subtle plate behind an uploaded logo when the page is dark, and paints nothing at all in light mode.
</Update>

<Update label="15 September 2026" tags={["Forms"]}>
  ## Rating and scale questions on forms

  You can now ask "how would you rate this, 1 to 5" — as stars, or as a numbered scale.

  <Note>
    An unanswered rating exports as **empty**, never as zero. A zero is a rating, and averaging a column of accidental zeroes is how a survey ends up looking far worse than it was.
  </Note>

  Answers export as numbers, so a column can be averaged or charted without anyone converting "4 stars" back into a 4.
</Update>

<Update label="15 September 2026" tags={["Plans","Fixed"]}>
  ## Adding a second campus told you to buy the wrong thing

  A church at its campus limit was told to upgrade to Ministry. **Ministry does not include a second campus** — every plan includes exactly one, and extra campuses are an add-on on every tier. So the advice pointed at a purchase that would not have helped.

  The button was also unpressable on a phone, so on touch nothing happened at all.

  **Both fixed.** The message now names the actual cost and offers the add-on.
</Update>

<Update label="14 September 2026" tags={["Accounting","Fixed"]}>
  ## Two screens were telling you something untrue about your own money

  Accounting and Giving statements both said that a gift you record in the CRM does not count towards their totals and will not reach a member's statement.

  **That has been false since we made manual recording write a real receipt.** Three other screens in Harvest already said it counts. These two said the opposite, and nobody noticed.

  **Both now say what actually happens:** a gift you record by hand reaches your accounting, your dashboard and that member's year-end statement, exactly like a card gift.

  <Warning>
    Proving that turned up something nobody knew: **a gift recorded against a contact with no email address can never appear on a giving statement.** Statements are grouped by email, so there is nobody to group it under — it is skipped.

    It still counts in your books. Both screens now say so, and it is worth an audit before you tell your congregation their statements are ready.
  </Warning>
</Update>

<Update label="14 September 2026" tags={["CRM","Fixed"]}>
  ## Deleting a contact did nothing

  A contact you deleted came straight back on the next refresh. The delete was aiming at the wrong record entirely for anyone who had a Harvest account, so it was quietly writing to a document that had never existed.

  **Fixed** — and while we were there:

  - **Deleting a contact now removes their activity timeline too**, instead of leaving it behind with nothing to attach to.
  - **Your receipts are never deleted.** Accounting and giving statements do not move when a contact goes.
  - **An admin can no longer delete their own account**, which previously had no guard at all. You could have locked yourself out of a church you were the only admin of.
  - **A failed delete now says so** instead of closing the dialog as though it had worked.

  The confirmation dialog also explains what deleting actually reaches, rather than only warning that it cannot be undone.
</Update>

<Update label="11 September 2026" tags={["Events", "Giving"]}>
## Paying for an event actually works

If your church took ticket payments, the registration page was not showing your payment links — so people registered and then had no way to pay you.

**Now:** the page shows the payment links you chose for that event, gives the attendee a reference code to put in the payment note, and lets them tell you they have paid. That claim lands in your inbox, and you confirm it after checking your own account.

This works for anyone with the link — they do not need a Harvest account, which matters for outreach where most people are not members yet.

We also removed old wording that promised to send people to a payment page that no longer existed.
</Update>

## A gift you record now counts everywhere

Recording a donation in the CRM used to update the CRM and nothing else. Your dashboard did not move and your books did not know about it.

**Now:** one entry updates your dashboard, creates the invoice in accounting, appears in that member's own giving history, and lands on their year-end statement. You enter it once.

<Warning>
  Gifts are matched to a member by their email address. If a contact has no email, the gift still counts for your church but the member will never see it. Harvest now tells you this on screen before you save.
</Warning>
</Update>
