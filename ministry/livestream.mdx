<Note>
  Both features are available on **Pro and Ministry**.
</Note>

## Livestream

Bring your stream into the member app so your congregation watches in one place rather than hunting for a link.

Add your stream and it appears in the member app when you are live. People who cannot be in the room — the ill, the travelling, the housebound — open the same app they already use for everything else.

<Tip>
  A note in the [community feed](/content/blog-and-community) pointing at the stream, pinned for the morning, does more for attendance than anything in the settings.
</Tip>

## Sermon notes

Give your congregation notes to follow during the message — an outline, the passages, the questions you want them sitting with afterwards.

Members can take their own notes alongside yours, and those stay in their account.

### Why it is worth the ten minutes

Someone who wrote something down on Sunday is far more likely to do something about it by Wednesday. Notes also give your small groups a starting point that everyone already has.

## Sharing a note to the stream

From [Notes](/content/blog-and-community), a document can be shared straight to your livestream — so an outline you wrote in the week reaches the screen without being retyped.
